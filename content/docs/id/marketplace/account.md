# Account

## Sign up

1. Open the app and start registration.
2. Verify phone via **OTP** (WhatsApp or SMS, depending on configuration).
3. Complete business profile fields.

## Sign in

Use username/email and password, or OTP where enabled.

## Suspended accounts

Suspended buyers see a blocking message and cannot place orders until the distributor or platform resolves the issue.
