**Account Settings** (`/settings`) mengatur profil, data perusahaan storefront, tim, locale, penagihan, dan keamanan.

## Tab pengaturan

| Tab | Fungsi |
|-----|--------|
| **General Info** | Profil pribadi |
| **Distributor Operations** | Perusahaan, pengiriman, data marketplace |
| **Features & Tours** | Ulang panduan interaktif |
| **Team & Sales** | Tim penjualan dan wilayah |
| **Language & Region** | Bahasa, wilayah, mata uang |
| **Billing & Payment** | Rekening dan alamat penagihan |
| **Security & Password** | Email dan kata sandi |

## General Info

**Personal Information:** foto, nama, email, telepon, bio, lokasi.

**Edit**, **Save**, **Cancel**.

## Distributor Operations

**Company Information:** logo, banner, nama perusahaan, registrasi, NPWP/tax ID, deskripsi.

Kontak: **Business Address**, **Business Phone**, **Business Email**.

**Shipping & Delivery Configurations:**

- **Shipping Options:** Self Pickup, Seller/Distributor Delivery, Buyer-arranged Courier
- **Pickup Address**
- **Preferred Delivery Time Slots**, **Preferred Delivery Days**
- Lead time pengiriman default

Nilai ini tampil di **Marketplace Dashboard** dan checkout pembeli.

**Show discounted price when buyers submit a PO**

Pembeli selalu melihat harga dasar katalog di kartu produk. Jika sakelar ini aktif, pesanan pembelian baru menampilkan tarif klien itu begitu mereka mengirim. Jika mati, pesanan tetap pada harga dasar katalog sampai Anda memakai **Send pricing to buyer** di pesanan. Hubungan pembeli yang sudah ada tidak menyalakan tarif dengan sendirinya.

Lihat [Penawaran](/id/docs/dashboard/offers) dan [Pesanan](/id/docs/dashboard/orders).

## Features & Tours

Tur ulang: Dashboard overview, Products, Orders, Offers, Marketplace, Logistics.

Status: **Completed**, **Skipped / closed**, **Not started**.

**Start tour**, **Reset**.

## Team & Sales

**Sales Team Overview**, target, progres.

**Sales Team Directory:** pencarian, **Filter by role**.

**Territory Management**.

Modal **Add Sales Team Member** / **Edit Sales Team Member**.

Terkait **Marketing → Teams** untuk konfigurasi penjualan di tingkat akun.

## Language & Region

**Language**, **Region**, **Currency** (mata uang bisa read-only).

## Billing & Payment

**Billing Account** — **Edit Account**.

**Billing Address** — **Edit Address**.

Bagian langganan dan invoice bisa disembunyikan hingga fitur penagihan aktif.

## Security & Password

**Email Address**, **Password** — **Create Password** / **Change Password**.

Jaga data perusahaan dan penagihan tetap mutakhir.
