Lakupak exists to help independent FMCG distributors in Indonesia **connect fragmented operations, coordinate sales more effectively, and run private B2B commerce**, with trust and commercial control intact.

## What "Lakupak" means

In Indonesian, *laku* describes products that sell well, turn over quickly, and succeed in the market. Trade moves when information, people, and activities stay connected, and when scarce sales time goes to opportunities supported by evidence.

## The problem we address

Independent wholesale FMCG distribution still runs into operational chaos:

- Products, customers, orders, pricing, and sales activity live in separate tools
- Spreadsheets, accounting software, ERP, WhatsApp, and notebooks do not share one picture
- Sales representatives depend on visits, chats, and memory to decide who to see and what to offer
- Buyer-specific prices and terms are hard to retrieve and apply consistently
- Managers lack a clear view of follow-ups, performance, and where opportunity remains

Underused products and low assortment penetration across eligible shops can follow from that fragmentation. They are consequences to investigate, not the whole problem. Distributors need a systematic way to unify commercial information, coordinate sales work, and execute through channels they control.

## Our mission

Help distributors reduce operational complexity, improve sales productivity, strengthen retailer relationships, and unlock more value from the networks they already have, without compromising control or trust.

Success looks like:

- One connected view of clients, catalog, orders, offers, and sales activity
- Sales time spent on clearer priorities instead of chasing information
- Private per-retailer pricing and commercial terms that stay consistent
- Authorised buyers ordering through a controlled Marketplace
- Outcomes that improve the next round of decisions

## What Lakupak is (and is not)

| Lakupak is | Lakupak is not |
|---------|-------------|
| An **AI-native B2B commerce and sales platform** | A consumer shopping app |
| Built for **independent Indonesian FMCG distributors** | A tool that only arrives if a CPG principal deploys it |
| Operational foundation plus commercial intelligence and controlled commerce | Indiscriminate push of every SKU to every shop |
| Distributor-controlled commercial data | An open price-war marketplace |
