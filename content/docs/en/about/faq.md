Answers to questions distributors, buyers, and partners often ask about Lakupak.

## General

### Is Lakupak software for distributors?

Yes. Lakupak is a **B2B commerce and sales platform**. Distributors run daily operations in **Dashboard**, and authorised buyers order in **Marketplace**. See [Mission and story](/en/docs/about/mission-and-story).

### Who owns the customer relationship?

The **distributor** owns the commercial relationship with retail buyers. Lakupak provides the channel and record-keeping; it does not interpose as the seller of record in the traditional sense.

### Is Lakupak only for Indonesia?

The product and language support are built for **Indonesia** wholesale and retail habits (warung, grosir, FMCG routes). Deployments may vary by partner; features in this documentation reflect the Lakupak product direction.

## For distributors

### Do I have to put all my buyers on the marketplace?

No. You control **which products are published** and which buyers engage digitally. Many distributors start with a subset of SKUs or key accounts, then expand.

### Can I keep using WhatsApp with buyers?

Yes. Lakupak reduces reliance on chat for **order lines and status**, but sales relationships can still use WhatsApp for relationship and negotiation. The goal is fewer errors on the order itself.

## For retail buyers

### Do I buy from Lakupak or from my distributor?

You buy from the **distributor** whose catalog you see. Lakupak is the ordering and tracking tool. Payment terms (transfer, COD) follow that distributor's rules.

### Can I compare prices from many distributors in one place?

Lakupak is not designed as an open cross-distributor price comparison site. You shop **within each distributor's storefront** you have access to.

### How do returns work?

Use in-app **Return/Refund** when the distributor's policy allows. The distributor resolves the case; Lakupak records the request and status.
