This page summarizes **business outcomes** by role. For step-by-step usage, open the module guides linked in each section.

## Distributors

| Area | Without structured tools | With Lakupak |
|------|--------------------------|----------|
| Ordering | Chats, screenshots, retyped lines | Structured orders in **Orders** with status |
| Catalog | Static PDFs or verbal price lists | Live **Products** with stock and publish |
| Customers | Memory and notebooks | **Clients** with history and segments |
| Promotions | Manual discounts per chat | **Offers** with dates and product rules |
| Delivery | Phone calls to drivers | **Logistics**, maps, and **Driver** app |
| Insight | End-of-month surprises | **Analytics** and Home KPIs |

Typical wins: fewer order errors, faster fulfillment, better retention of key warung accounts, clearer view of slow-moving SKUs.

Doc hub: [Dashboard](/en/docs/dashboard/overview).

## Retail buyers

| Area | Without Lakupak | With Lakupak |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| Discovery | Call sales rep or wait for visit | **Browse**, **Search**, **Categories** anytime |
| Reorder | Re-type the same list in chat | **Buy Again**, favourites, cart history |
| Status | "Where is my order?" messages | **Orders** tab and notifications |
| Promotions | Hear about deals late | **Special Offers** and distributor stores |
| Trust | Informal only | Store profile, policies, **Follow** |

Typical wins: less time ordering, fewer stockouts before peak hours, clearer payment and delivery expectations.

Doc hub: [Marketplace](/en/docs/marketplace/overview).

## Drivers

| Area | Without Lakupak | With Lakupak |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| Route | Paper lists or ad hoc calls | **Tasks** calendar and map |
| Proof | Photos in personal gallery | **Proof of Delivery** attached to order |
| Issues | Verbal report later | **Report failure** with reasons and photos |
| Status | Dispatcher calls each stop | Tap **Start Delivery** / **Complete Delivery** |

Typical wins: less back-and-forth with dispatch, clearer accountability, faster closes at each stop.

Doc hub: [Driver](/en/docs/driver/overview).

## Shared benefits across the chain

- **One order record** from checkout to delivery
- **Notifications** when status changes
- **Audit trail** for disputes (POD, cancellations, returns)
- **Room to grow** into analytics and campaigns without new tools
