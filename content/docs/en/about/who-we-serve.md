Lakupak sits in the **middle of the FMCG and general wholesale supply chain**, connecting the party that stocks and supplies with the small shops that sell to end consumers.

## Distributors (FMCG and wholesalers)

Typical users run **Lakupak Dashboard** on the web.

They may be:

- Regional FMCG distributors
- General wholesalers serving warungs and mini markets
- Brands or sales teams that want structured B2B ordering

### Common challenges

- Products, customers, orders, and pricing scattered across chats, spreadsheets, and separate systems
- Buyer-specific prices and commercial terms that are hard to retrieve and apply consistently
- Sales teams that struggle to prioritise who to visit and what to offer next
- Weak day-to-day visibility into follow-ups, order status, and commercial performance

### What Lakupak helps with

- **Clients** (buyers and suppliers) with history in one place
- Central **Products** catalog with variants, stock, segment pricing, and publish control
- **Orders** pipeline from placement to fulfillment
- **Offers** for private per-retailer rates without manual price lists
- Private **Marketplace** storefront for authorised retailers
- **Sales** workflows where the module is available, plus thin outcome metrics and Lakupak AI

Start: [Dashboard overview](/en/docs/dashboard/overview).

## Retail buyers (warungs, kiosks, small shops)

Typical users run **Lakupak Marketplace** on mobile.

They need:

- Reliable restock from familiar distributors
- Bulk quantities and clear minimum orders
- Visibility when goods are coming
- Fair terms with suppliers they already trust

### What Lakupak helps with

- Browse and **search** published catalogs
- **Cart and checkout** with delivery and payment choices
- **Order tracking**, payment, returns where allowed
- **Follow** favorite distributors and see **offers**

Start: [Marketplace overview](/en/docs/marketplace/overview).

## How the sides connect

```text
Distributor (Dashboard)  →  clients, catalog, offers, orders, publish
        ↓
Buyer (Marketplace)      →  discover, order, pay, track
```

Both sides see the **same order** at different stages. See [How orders flow](/en/docs/platform/order-flow).
