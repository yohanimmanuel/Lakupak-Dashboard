# Orders

## Order list

The **Orders** tab lists active and past orders with status badges.

## Order detail

- View line items and delivery info
- **Pay** when payment is pending
- **Cancel** or **Return/refund** when allowed by policy
- Open **Delivery information** and map for tracking
