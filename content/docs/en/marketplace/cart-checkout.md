# Cart and checkout

## Cart

Add line items with quantities (often by carton). Cart syncs via GraphQL while you shop.

## Checkout

1. Review cart and delivery address.
2. Confirm order; seller bank details may display for transfer payment.
3. Complete checkout to create the order in the distributor Dashboard.

## After checkout

Use **Payment success** and order detail screens to complete payment steps when required by the distributor.
