Manage profile, delivery addresses, language, notifications, and support links from **Settings** and the Account tab.

## Settings hub

Path: `/settings`. Title: **Settings**.

### Account and addresses

| Row | Destination |
|-----|-------------|
| **Account & Security** | Profile edit (`editprofile`) |
| **My Addresses** | Address list and **New Address** |
| **Linked Payment Methods** | Placeholder when not enabled |

Open **Settings** from the gear icon on the Account tab header.

### Language and notifications

| Row | Destination |
|-----|-------------|
| **Notification Settings** | `/settings/notifications` |
| **Language Settings** | `/settings/language` (shows **English** or **Bahasa Indonesia**) |

**Language** screen: **Choose your app language**.

Default app language may follow device locale; Indonesian (`id`) is common for Indonesia deployments.

### Help and legal

| Row | Destination |
|-----|-------------|
| **Help Center** | Support content |
| **Community Rules** | Community guidelines |
| **Policies** | **Security & Privacy** |

Also available from login/signup overflow: **Security & Privacy**, **Help Center**.

Footer: **Log Out**.

## Profile editing

From **Account & Security** or Account profile row, edit:

- Username, name, bio, phone, email
- Store name, country
- Profile photo

**My Addresses** powers the **Delivery Address** block at checkout.

## Messages

Path: `/messages`. Title: **Messages**.

Empty: **No messages yet** / **Chat with distributors from product pages to see them here.**

Chats open WhatsApp using the distributor contact from **Chat Now** on product pages.
