Use this page to pick the right experience and complete first setup steps. For product framing, see [Mission and story](/en/docs/about/mission-and-story).

## Distributors (Dashboard)

1. Register on the Lakupak website and sign in to the Dashboard.
2. Complete **Settings → Distributor Operations** (company profile, delivery preferences, and storefront-related settings as available).
3. Add retailers under **Clients → Buyers** (and suppliers if needed).
4. Build your catalog under **Products** (variants, pricing, stock, images).
5. Configure **Offers** for per-retailer product rates where needed.
6. Record and fulfill **Orders**, and publish products so authorised buyers can order in Marketplace.

Doc hub: [Dashboard overview](/en/docs/dashboard/overview).

## Retail buyers (Marketplace)

1. Join with a valid distributor referral or membership code.
2. Browse the authorised distributor catalog and applicable offers.
3. Place carton or bulk orders and track status.

Doc hub: [Marketplace overview](/en/docs/marketplace/overview).
