Use this page to pick the right app and complete your first setup steps. For background on Lakupak as a business platform, see [About Lakupak](/en/docs/about/mission-and-story).

## Distributors (Dashboard)

1. Register on the Lakupak website and sign in to the Dashboard.
2. Complete **Settings → Distributor Operations** (company profile, delivery preferences).
3. Add products under **Products**, then **Publish** items to the marketplace.
4. Invite buyers or share your storefront; manage incoming orders under **Orders**.
5. Configure **Logistics** fleets and driver accounts for delivery.

Doc hub: [Dashboard overview](/en/docs/dashboard/overview).

## Retail buyers (Marketplace)

1. Install **Lakupak Marketplace** from your app store (or internal distribution).
2. Create an account and complete registration.
3. Browse or search products, add to cart, and checkout.
4. Track orders under the **Orders** tab.

Doc hub: [Marketplace overview](/en/docs/marketplace/overview).

## Drivers (Driver app)

1. Your distributor creates your driver account in **Dashboard → Logistics**.
2. Install **Lakupak Driver** and log in with issued credentials.
3. Open **Tasks**, select a fleet, and update delivery status per stop.

Doc hub: [Driver overview](/en/docs/driver/overview).
