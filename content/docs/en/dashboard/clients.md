# Clients

Manage B2B relationships in two areas:

## Buyers

Retail accounts that purchase from you on the marketplace. View profiles, order history, and restore deleted buyers from **Deleted buyers** when needed.

## Suppliers

Upstream suppliers for your catalog. Track contact details and purchasing relationships.
