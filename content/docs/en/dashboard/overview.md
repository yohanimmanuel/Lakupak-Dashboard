# Dashboard overview

The distributor Dashboard is the control center for catalog, orders, CRM, analytics, marketplace presence, and logistics.

## Sign in

Use **Login** with email and password. New distributors register via **Get Started**, then land on **Home**.

## Sidebar navigation

- **Home** — KPIs and shortcuts
- **Clients** — Buyers and suppliers
- **Products**, **Orders**, **Offers**
- **Marketing**, **Analytics**
- **Marketplace**, **Logistics**, **Settings**

## Product tours

Open **Settings → Features & Tours** to replay guided walkthroughs. A welcome prompt may appear on first login.
