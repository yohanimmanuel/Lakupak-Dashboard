The **Marketplace Dashboard** (`/marketplace`) is for distributors: it shows how your storefront performs on the buyer app. It is not the buyer mobile app itself.

## Time range and refresh

**Filters:** Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Last year.

**Refresh** reloads marketplace metrics.

## Quick stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Published Products** | Live on marketplace |
| **Total Products** | Full catalog count |
| **Unpublished** | Draft items ready to publish |

## Distributor profile

**Distributor information** summarizes what buyers see:

- **Buyers** (unique buyers) and **Followers**
- Company name, **Marketplace URL** (`laku.marketplace/…`), phone, email, address
- **Shipping options:** Self Pickup (Buyer collects), Seller / Distributor Delivery, Buyer-arranged Courier (**Available** / **Not offered**)
- **Default lead time** and **Return policy**

**Edit settings** opens **Settings → Distributor Operations** for changes.

## Performance metrics

### Overall metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Total Sales** | Marketplace revenue in period |
| **Total Views** | Product and store views |
| **Conversion Rate** | Views to purchases |

### Customer journey

Funnel: **Views** → **Added to Cart** → **Purchases**.

### Product performance table

Columns: **Product**, **Views**, **Cart**, **Favorites**, **Sold**, **Reviews**, **Sales**, **Conv %**.

**Sort by:** Views, Sales, Revenue.

Row click opens **Detailed Performance Analytics** with reviews and product-level metrics.

## Published products list

**Published Products** section lists live cards with views, likes, and sales.

| Action | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Manage Products** | Go to Products catalog |
| **View Details →** | Open product catalog view |

Empty: **No Published Products** with **Go to Products**.
