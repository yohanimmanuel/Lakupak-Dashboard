The **Marketplace Dashboard** (`/marketplace`) is for distributors. It shows how your private storefront performs for authorised buyers. It is not the buyer mobile app itself. For the buyer experience, see [Marketplace overview](/en/docs/marketplace/overview).

## Time range and refresh

**Filters:** Last 7 days | Last 30 days | Last 90 days | Last year

**Refresh** reloads marketplace metrics for the selected range.

## Quick stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Published Products** | Live on the buyer marketplace |
| **Total Products** | Full catalog count |
| **Unpublished** | Not yet published |

## Distributor information

Summarises what buyers see about your storefront:

- **Customers / Buyers**, **Unique buyers**, **Followers**
- Company identity, **Marketplace URL**, phone, email, address
- **Shipping options** (Self Pickup, Seller / Distributor Delivery, Buyer-arranged Courier) as Available or Not offered
- Pickup, default lead time, return policy

**Edit settings** opens **Settings → Distributor Operations**.

## Marketplace performance

### Overall metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Total Sales** | Marketplace revenue in the period |
| **Total Views** | Product and store views |
| **Conversion Rate** | Views to purchases |

### Customer journey

Funnel: **Views** → **Added to Cart** → **Purchases**.

### Product performance table

Columns: **Product** | **Views** | **Cart** | **Favorites** | **Sold** | **Reviews** | **Sales** | **Conv %**

**Sort by:** Views | Sales | Revenue

Row click opens detailed performance (analytics and reviews tabs for that product).

## Published products

**Published Products** lists live listing cards with views, likes, and sales.

| Action | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Manage Products** | Open the Products catalog |
| **Go to Products** | Same catalog when the list is empty |

Empty state: **No Published Products**.

## Data notes

Metrics come from marketplace activity for your account. Very large catalogs may load in capped batches; refresh after publishing or unpublishing products to update counts.
