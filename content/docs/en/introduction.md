**Laku** is a digital platform designed to modernize wholesale trade in Indonesia by connecting distributors and small retailers within one trusted ecosystem. In Indonesian, *"laku"* means something that sells well, moves quickly, and succeeds in the market. That meaning reflects the platform's mission: helping goods move faster, transactions happen more smoothly, and businesses grow sustainably through technology.

At its core, Laku combines two ideas in one system. First, it gives distributors a private digital operating platform to manage products, inventory, orders, customers, and delivery. Many still depend on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and informal ordering. Laku replaces those fragmented workflows with a structured dashboard that acts as a central operating system for daily business, so teams can track performance, handle orders efficiently, and build long-term relationships using data rather than memory.

Second, Laku works as a B2B marketplace for warungs, kiosks, small shops, and independent retailers. Buyers can discover products and place wholesale orders without manually contacting many suppliers. The goal is not to disrupt distributor relationships but to strengthen them through simpler, faster, and more reliable procurement.

Unlike open marketplaces that often fuel price wars, Laku is a **controlled marketplace**. Distributors keep ownership of pricing, customer relationships, and operational control. The platform facilitates transactions and digital workflows without exposing sensitive competitive information or forcing unhealthy competition.

Laku also connects the execution layer through delivery coordination. Distributors assign orders to drivers, monitor progress, and update statuses in one system, creating visibility from listing to final delivery. This structured data becomes the foundation for smarter analytics and automation over time.

In essence, Laku is neither purely a marketplace nor purely SaaS. It is a **B2B commerce operating platform** built to digitize how goods move between distributors and small retailers in Indonesia, making buying simpler, selling faster, and relationships more scalable.

## What Laku includes

- **Laku Dashboard**: web app for distributors to manage catalog, orders, clients, logistics, and analytics.
- **Laku Marketplace**: mobile app for buyers to browse, order in bulk, and track deliveries.
- **Laku Driver**: mobile app for fleet drivers to execute deliveries and update status.
