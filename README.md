
# Lakupak!

### AI-Native B2B Commerce & Sales Platform for FMCG Distributors

**A Smarter Future for Every Distributor.**

## Overview

Lakupak is an AI-native B2B Commerce and Sales platform built to solve **operational chaos in Indonesia's independent FMCG distribution industry**, by connecting fragmented business operations into one intelligent platform that enables more efficient management and smarter commercial decision-making.

Independent distributors already manage extensive product portfolios (50-1000 SKUs), established retailer networks, and active sales operations. However, the information and activities required to run these businesses are frequently scattered across spreadsheets, accounting software, ERP systems, WhatsApp, and manual workflows.

Lakupak connects distributor operations, AI-powered commercial intelligence, and private B2B commerce within one integrated platform, helping distributors improve operational visibility, coordinate sales activities, make informed commercial decisions, and unlock greater value from their existing distribution networks.

Our mission is to transform fragmented distribution operations into an intelligent, connected commercial environment.

**Who should we reach? What should we offer? When should we act?**

### Unique Value Proposition

For independent FMCG distributors in Indonesia managing diverse product portfolios and established networks of traditional retailers, Lakupak addresses the operational complexity created by disconnected business information, manual coordination, fragmented sales workflows, and limited commercial visibility.

Although distributors already use tools such as ERP systems, accounting software, spreadsheets, and messaging applications, these systems often operate independently, require additional services to integrate that burdens cost and hurt distributor margins. As a result, distributors struggle to connect customer relationships, product information, orders, pricing arrangements, and sales activities into a coherent commercial workflow.

Lakupak unifies essential distributor operations within a connected vertical SaaS platform, complemented by AI-powered commercial intelligence and a distributor-controlled private B2B marketplace.

Rather than simply digitizing existing processes, Lakupak aims to help distributors understand their businesses more clearly, prioritize commercial activities, and execute sales decisions more effectively.

**Ultimately, Lakupak helps distributors reduce operational complexity, improve sales productivity, strengthen retailer relationships, and unlock the full commercial potential of their existing distribution networks.**

## Problem Scope

### Operational Chaos in Independent FMCG Distribution

Independent FMCG distributors in Indonesia operate within a complex commercial environment involving extensive product portfolios, retailer networks, customer-specific pricing arrangements, inventory information, orders, and sales representatives.

Despite the availability of digital tools, many distributors continue to manage these interconnected activities through fragmented systems and manual processes.

Product information may exist in spreadsheets, customer relationships in WhatsApp conversations, transactions in accounting software, and sales activities in separate records.

Each tool may perform its individual function, but the absence of connected information makes commercial coordination and decision-making increasingly difficult.

**The fundamental problem is not that distributors cannot sell their products. It is that the way they manage their operations limits how effectively they can utilize their existing resources and capture their full commercial potential.**

This creates four interconnected operational challenges:

### 1. Fragmented Business Information

Distributors manage products, customers, orders, pricing arrangements, inventory information, and sales activities across different tools and systems.

Without a unified operational environment, managers must manually gather and reconcile information to understand business performance.

This fragmentation limits visibility, increases administrative complexity, and makes it difficult to identify which areas of the business require attention.

### 2. Inefficient Sales Activities

Sales representatives depend heavily on physical retailer visits, messaging applications, manual order collection, and individual experience.

Their daily activities require decisions about which retailers to approach, what products to introduce, which orders to follow up on, and when to take action.

Without connected information and structured sales workflows, these activities become repetitive and difficult to coordinate.

Sales representatives may spend valuable time retrieving information, handling administrative tasks, and repeating routine interactions instead of focusing on higher-value commercial opportunities.

### 3. Complex Buyer-Specific Pricing and Commercial Terms

Different retailers frequently operate under different negotiated prices, discounts, purchasing arrangements, and commercial terms.

Managing these relationships across separate records and communication channels creates unnecessary complexity.

Sales teams may struggle to retrieve accurate account-specific information, coordinate promotions, or ensure that commercial arrangements are consistently applied.

This increases the risk of administrative errors, inconsistent offers, and inefficient customer management.

### 4. Limited Operational and Commercial Visibility

Distributors may have access to transaction records and sales reports without having a consolidated understanding of their commercial operations.

Managers may struggle to determine:

- Which retailers require attention or follow-up.
- Which sales activities are producing meaningful outcomes.
- Which products are performing well across different customer segments.
- Where potential commercial opportunities remain unexplored.
- How effectively existing sales resources are being utilized.

Without connected information, these decisions remain heavily dependent on manual analysis, individual judgment, and fragmented reporting.

### The Consequences: Underutilized Distribution Potential

Operational chaos affects more than administrative efficiency.

It limits how effectively distributors coordinate their existing resources, serve retailer networks, and convert commercial opportunities into sales.

Potential consequences include:

- Underutilized distribution networks.
- Product opportunities remaining undiscovered.
- Repetitive and inefficient sales activities.
- Difficulties coordinating buyer-specific pricing and commercial terms.
- Limited visibility into retailer purchasing behavior.
- Relevant products remaining under-adopted across retailer networks.
- Missed orders and unrealized account growth.
- Greater dependence on individual employees' experience and manual coordination.

Low assortment penetration and product underutilization are therefore potential symptoms of broader operational inefficiencies, rather than the sole underlying problem.

Not every product needs to be sold to every retailer. Demand, product suitability, pricing, availability, and customer preferences all influence purchasing decisions.

Lakupak focuses on improving the operational and commercial processes that allow distributors to identify and pursue genuine opportunities.

**The problem is not simply moving products. It is connecting the information, people, and activities needed to move them effectively.**

## Solution

Lakupak connects distributor operations, AI-powered commercial intelligence, and private B2B commerce within one integrated platform.

The solution is designed to reduce operational fragmentation, improve business visibility, and help distributors make more effective commercial decisions.

Lakupak follows a connected commercial intelligence framework:

### UNIFY → IDENTIFY → MATCH → EXECUTE → LEARN

**1. Unify**

Bring product catalogues, retailer relationships, purchasing histories, pricing arrangements, orders, and sales activities into a connected operational environment.

Establish a consistent commercial data foundation that reduces reliance on disconnected records and manual information retrieval.

**2. Identify**

Analyze available operational and commercial information to identify inefficiencies, customer activity patterns, missed follow-ups, declining purchases, and potential sales opportunities.

Help distributors understand which areas require attention and where their existing resources can be utilized more effectively.

**3. Match**

Connect relevant products, retailers, and commercial activities using purchasing histories, customer characteristics, pricing arrangements, stock availability, and available business information.

Support more informed sales decisions without assuming that every product is suitable for every retailer.

**4. Execute**

Translate commercial insights into actionable workflows through sales assignments, targeted offers, customer follow-ups, product recommendations, and private B2B commerce.

Help sales teams coordinate activities more efficiently and reduce unnecessary manual effort.

**5. Learn**

Evaluate commercial outcomes through recorded activities, retailer responses, orders, product performance, and repeat purchasing behavior.

Use accumulated information to improve future recommendations and operational decisions as reliable data becomes available.

### AI-Native by Design

Lakupak is designed to integrate intelligence directly into distributor operations and commercial workflows rather than treating AI as a standalone chatbot.

Its intended AI capabilities support:

- Commercial opportunity identification.
- Sales activity prioritization.
- Retailer purchasing analysis.
- Product-to-retailer recommendations.
- Reorder and customer follow-up intelligence.
- Operational performance insights.
- Workflow automation and decision support.

Recommendations must remain grounded in available data, respect commercial constraints, and support human oversight.

### Distributor-Controlled Commerce

Lakupak is built around the existing relationships between distributors and their retailers.

Distributors retain control over their:

- Product catalogues.
- Retailer relationships.
- Customer-specific pricing.
- Commercial terms.
- Product availability.
- Business and transaction data.

The private B2B marketplace complements existing distribution relationships rather than replacing them.

Lakupak's initial focus is helping independent FMCG distributors connect their operations and improve commercial execution.

Over time, the same infrastructure may support broader commerce opportunities, including potential consumer-facing services.

## Key Security & Trust Principles

Lakupak is built around the principle that connected operations and commercial intelligence must create value without compromising distributor trust or control.

- **Data Privacy and Confidentiality:** Protect sensitive business information, including product catalogues, pricing, customer records, purchasing histories, and sales performance.

- **Distributor Data Isolation:** Keep each distributor's business information separated from other distributors. One distributor must not be able to access another distributor's private customers, transactions, or commercial insights.

- **Controlled Marketplace Access:** Give authorized retailers access to their relevant distributor's private storefront, products, and applicable pricing rather than exposing commercial information through an unrestricted marketplace.

- **Distributor Control over Pricing and Relationships:** Allow distributors to retain control over pricing policies, retailer relationships, product availability, and negotiated commercial terms.

- **Responsible AI and Recommendations:** Ground recommendations in available information, respect access permissions, and present commercial opportunities as estimates rather than guaranteed outcomes.

- **Trust-Centered Adoption:** Make security, transparency, operational reliability, and clear data-access boundaries central to Lakupak's product design and distributor acquisition strategy.

These principles define Lakupak's intended security and trust requirements. Actual implementation and enforcement must be validated against the platform's code, infrastructure, and operational practices.

## Core Features

### 1. Distributor Dashboard

A centralized workspace for distributors to manage, coordinate, and monitor their commercial operations.

- **Client Management:** Organize retailer and supplier records, contact information, business details, and relationship histories.

- **Product Management:** Maintain product catalogues, pricing, product details, categories, and stock information.

- **Order Management:** Manage incoming marketplace orders, transaction information, and fulfillment status.

- **Per-Retailer Offers:** Build and configure discounts, promotions, negotiated prices, and volume-based commercial terms for specific buyers.

- **Sales Management:** Organize sales representatives, campaigns, leads, customer engagement, and follow-up activities.

- **Analytics:** Monitor business performance, order activity, product performance, retailer purchasing patterns, and relevant commercial indicators.

The dashboard serves as Lakupak's operational foundation, connecting business information that would otherwise remain distributed across separate tools.

### 2. Product–Retailer Intelligence

Help distributors understand product performance, retailer purchasing behavior, and potential commercial opportunities within their existing distribution networks.

- **Assortment Visibility:** Examine how products are distributed and purchased across relevant retailer segments.

- **Opportunity Identification:** Identify potentially overlooked products, customer purchasing gaps, and opportunities requiring further investigation.

- **Retailer Matching:** Identify retailers who may be suitable for particular products based on purchasing histories, product relationships, and business rules.

- **Opportunity Prioritization:** Rank potential commercial opportunities using factors such as customer suitability, stock availability, pricing, and previous purchasing behavior.

- **Performance Evaluation:** Compare commercial recommendations and observed sales outcomes over time as sufficient data becomes available.

Product–retailer intelligence is one component of Lakupak's broader commercial intelligence capabilities.

Low product penetration does not automatically indicate unmet demand. Recommendations should account for product suitability, availability, pricing, and other commercial constraints.

### 3. Sales Conversion and Follow-up

Help sales teams coordinate commercial activities, reduce repetitive manual work, and focus their limited time on actionable priorities.

- **Lead Management:** Organize prospective and existing retailer opportunities.

- **Follow-up Tracking:** Monitor outstanding sales tasks, customer interactions, and activities requiring attention.

- **Campaign Management:** Coordinate targeted promotions, commercial offers, and sales initiatives.

- **Sales Activity Visibility:** Help managers understand recorded sales activities, assignments, and follow-up progress.

- **Opportunity Execution:** Connect relevant commercial insights with salesperson outreach, retailer engagement, and ordering activities.

Sales conversion and follow-up tools help distributors translate operational information into coordinated commercial execution.

The objective is not simply to increase the number of sales activities, but to improve how effectively those activities are managed and prioritized.

### 4. Private B2B Retailer Marketplace

A controlled digital ordering channel connecting participating distributors with their authorized retail customers.

- **Product Discovery:** Allow retailers to browse products available through their relevant distributors.

- **Distributor-Specific Catalogues:** Present products and applicable prices according to retailer access and commercial arrangements.

- **Carton and Bulk Ordering:** Support the bulk purchasing requirements of FMCG retailers.

- **Direct Ordering:** Allow retailers to submit orders digitally instead of relying entirely on manual order-taking.

- **Order Visibility:** Provide access to relevant order details and status information.

The marketplace complements distributor relationships and existing sales operations rather than replacing them.

Product availability, pricing, and ordering rules remain subject to each distributor's configuration and business policies.

### 5. Analytics and Continuous Improvement

Transform accumulated operational and commercial information into insights that support better business management and decision-making.

- **Operational Performance:** Monitor relevant business activities, transaction patterns, and commercial workflows.

- **Product Performance:** Understand product sales, purchasing trends, and potential areas of underutilization.

- **Retailer Purchasing Patterns:** Examine purchasing frequency, order histories, and customer activity.

- **Sales Opportunity Tracking:** Monitor the progression from identified opportunities to commercial activities and completed orders.

- **Outcome Evaluation:** Assess whether recommendations, campaigns, and operational improvements are associated with meaningful business results.

The quality of insights depends on data completeness, reliability, and consistent activity tracking.

Observed improvements following a recommendation do not, by themselves, prove that the recommendation caused the outcome.

## Business Model

Lakupak follows a distributor-focused SaaS and transaction commission model.

### 1. Distributor SaaS Subscriptions

Distributors can subscribe to plans suited to their business needs, operational complexity, and scale.

Potential plan differentiation includes:

- **Free:** Access to essential tools for getting started.

- **Starter:** Core distributor management and operational tools for smaller businesses.

- **Business:** Expanded commercial management, sales intelligence, analytics, and automation capabilities.

- **Enterprise:** Advanced configuration, integrations, access management, and support for larger distribution operations.

The proposed Starter subscription begins at approximately Rp999,000 per month.

Exact feature limits, prices, and subscription entitlements should be defined and validated before commercial launch.

### 2. Marketplace Transaction Commission

Lakupak may charge a commission on eligible orders placed through its private B2B marketplace.

The proposed transaction fee is 0.25% on eligible completed marketplace orders, subject to commercial validation.

The fee applies to marketplace-facilitated transactions rather than automatically applying to all offline distributor sales recorded in the dashboard.

The final commission structure, eligibility rules, and any subscription-tier adjustments should be validated against distributor margins, transaction economics, and willingness to pay.

Lakupak's commercial sustainability depends on demonstrating measurable improvements that justify subscription and applicable transaction fees.

## Technical Architecture

Lakupak's intended architecture connects the distributor dashboard, retailer marketplace, backend services, and commercial data layer.

### Technology Stack

- **Web Dashboard:** Next.js and React for the distributor-facing management interface.

- **Mobile Marketplace:** React Native with Expo for the retailer-facing mobile experience.

- **Backend Services:** Node.js with GraphQL and REST API patterns for application services and data exchange.

- **Database:** PostgreSQL for structured operational and commercial data.

- **Real-Time Capabilities:** WebSockets and/or Firebase services where required for supported real-time features.

- **Authentication and Authorization:** Authentication flows and role-based access controls for appropriate user access.

- **External Integrations:** WhatsApp Business API and other relevant services where configured and supported.

- **Containerization:** Docker for consistent development and deployment environments.

The stack above describes the intended or stated technology choices. The exact services and integrations used in production should be confirmed against the current repository and deployment configuration.

### Architecture Principles

- **Modularity:** Separate application responsibilities so features can evolve without unnecessary coupling.

- **Data Integrity:** Maintain consistent relationships between distributors, retailers, products, inventory, orders, and sales activities.

- **Tenant Isolation:** Enforce distributor-level data boundaries across application and database access.

- **Access Control:** Apply authorization checks on the backend, not only in the user interface.

- **Scalability:** Structure services and data access to support growth in distributors, retailers, products, transactions, and commercial activities.

- **Operational Connectivity:** Ensure core business information can be connected across relevant distributor workflows.

- **AI Readiness:** Build a reliable operational and commercial data foundation before introducing advanced AI-driven recommendations and automation.
