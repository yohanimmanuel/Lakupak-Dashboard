# Lakupak!

## Overview

Lakupak is an **AI-native B2B Commerce and Sales platform** built to help independent FMCG distributors in Indonesia unlock the potential of their product portfolios and grow their retailer networks.

**Unique Value Proposition**

For independent FMCG distributors in Indonesia managing diverse product portfolios and large networks of traditional retailers, that experience limited visibility into which products are suitable for which retailers, causing potentially valuable SKUs to remain underpenetrated while sales teams rely heavily on familiar products, fragmented purchasing data and manual sales activities.

Lakupak aims to unify distributor and retailer data, identify assortment gaps, match relevant products to suitable retailers, and activate these opportunities through a distributor-controlled B2B storefront, targeted promotions and product trial bundles.

Ultimately, helping distributors expand relevant product adoption, improve sales productivity and generate more value from their existing product portfolios.

### Problem Scope

**Product Portfolio Underutilisation and Low Assortment Penetration**

Independent FMCG distributors often manage hundreds of products across hundreds of retailers. Yet, established bestsellers tend to dominate repeat orders, while other potentially valuable products struggle to reach the retailers most likely to buy them.

This creates four interconnected challenges:

- **Limited Product Penetration:** Distributors may have products that reach only a small share of their eligible retailer network, without a clear understanding of where additional demand might exist.
- **Limited Opportunity Visibility:** Sales teams cannot thoroughly evaluate every product against every retailer. They may continue promoting familiar bestsellers while suitable product–retailer opportunities remain unexplored.
- **Fragmented Commercial Data:** Product catalogs, retailer relationships, purchasing history, offers, and sales activities are often spread across different systems and manual workflows, making it difficult to identify meaningful patterns.
- **Inefficient Sales Activities:** Sales representatives have limited time and attention to engage each retailer and present the full range of relevant products. As a result, interactions may be too brief or infrequent, suitable products may go unpresented, and potential sales opportunities may remain unexplored.

The problem is not that every product must sell more. It is that distributors lack a systematic way to distinguish genuine growth opportunities from products with limited demand, identify suitable retailers, and make limited sales interactions more productive.

### Solution

Lakupak connects **distributor operations, AI-powered sales intelligence, and private B2B commerce** in one platform.

- **Unify:** Bring retailer relationships, product catalogs, inventory, orders, offers, and sales activities into a connected operational environment.
- **Identify:** Use available commercial data to examine product penetration, uncover assortment gaps, and identify potentially valuable product–retailer combinations.
- **Prioritise:** Apply AI-assisted analysis and business rules to rank opportunities based on factors such as purchasing history, retailer suitability, product relationships, pricing, and stock availability.
- **Activate:** Help sales teams act on promising opportunities through targeted product recommendations, promotions, and retailer follow-ups, making sales interactions more focused and productive.
- **Convert:** Connect the sales process to a private B2B marketplace where authorised retailers can discover products, view their applicable offers, and place orders directly.
- **Learn:** Use available product exposure, retailer responses, orders, and repeat purchases to evaluate outcomes and improve future recommendations as reliable data accumulates.

Lakupak is designed to be AI-native, with intelligence integrated into commerce and sales workflows rather than confined to a standalone chatbot. AI helps distributors make better-informed decisions, while recommendations remain grounded in available data and must not be treated as guaranteed demand.

Built for the realities of Indonesian FMCG distribution, Lakupak allows distributors to retain control over their retailer relationships, pricing, and commercial data through private storefronts and appropriate access controls.

The initial focus is helping independent FMCG distributors better utilise their product portfolios across existing retailers. Over time, the same distribution network may support broader commerce opportunities, including potential consumer-facing services.

## Key Security & Trust Principles

Lakupak is built around the principle that **commercial intelligence must create value without compromising distributor trust or control**.

- **Data Privacy and Confidentiality:** Protect sensitive business information, including product catalogs, pricing, customer records, purchasing history, and sales performance.
- **Distributor Data Isolation:** Keep each distributor's business data separated from other distributors' data. One distributor must not be able to access another distributor's private customers, transactions, or commercial insights.
- **Controlled Marketplace Access:** Give authorised retailers access to the relevant distributor's private storefront, products, and applicable pricing rather than exposing all distributors' commercial information through an unrestricted marketplace.
- **Distributor Control over Pricing and Relationships:** Allow distributors to retain control over their pricing policies, retailer relationships, product availability, and commercial terms.
- **Responsible AI and Recommendations:** Ground recommendations in available data, respect access permissions, and present potential opportunities as estimates rather than guaranteed sales outcomes.
- **Trust-Centred Adoption:** Make security, transparency, and clear data-access boundaries central to Lakupak's product design and distributor acquisition strategy.

These principles define Lakupak's intended security and trust requirements. Actual implementation and enforcement must be validated against the platform's code, infrastructure, and operational practices.

## Why Lakupak Is Different

Lakupak is designed specifically around the commercial realities of independent FMCG distributors, where sales opportunities depend not only on finding buyers but also understanding which products are suitable for which retailers. It goes beyond traditional distributor management software and standalone B2B marketplaces. It connects operational data, AI-driven sales intelligence, and digital commerce in one platform, helping independent FMCG distributors understand where product opportunities exist, decide which actions to take, and convert those opportunities into retailer orders.

- **AI-powered product portfolio intelligence:** Identify products with low penetration across eligible retailers and examine whether there are credible opportunities to expand their reach.
- **Product–retailer matching:** Use available purchasing history, retailer characteristics, product relationships, pricing, and other relevant signals to prioritise potentially suitable product–retailer combinations.
- **Connected operational foundation:** Connect product, retailer, inventory, order, offer, and sales activity data so recommendations can be evaluated in the context of actual distributor operations.
- **AI-assisted sales activation:** Help distributor teams prioritise which retailers to approach, which products to introduce, and which actions to take next.
- **Private B2B commerce marketplace:** Give authorised retailers a digital channel to discover and order products from their distributors, complementing existing relationships rather than replacing them.
- **Distributor-controlled commercial data:** Keep customer relationships, negotiated prices, and company-specific information protected through appropriate access controls and tenant isolation.
- **Continuous learning:** Use observed product exposure, retailer responses, orders, and repeat purchases to evaluate recommendations and improve future prioritisation as reliable data accumulates.

Low product penetration does not automatically mean unmet demand. A product may be unsuitable for a retailer, poorly priced, unavailable, or genuinely unpopular. Lakupak's objective is to help distributors investigate these differences and focus on opportunities supported by evidence, rather than encourage indiscriminate selling across every SKU.

## Core Features

### 1. Distributor Dashboard

A central workspace for distributors to manage and monitor their commercial operations.

- **Client Management:** Organise retailer and supplier records, contact information, business details, and relationship history.
- **Product Management:** Maintain product catalogs, pricing, product details, and stock information.
- **Order Management:** Track incoming marketplace orders and their fulfilment status.
- **Per Retailer Offer:** Build and configure discounts, promotions, and volume-based terms for specific buyers.
- **Sales Management:** Organise sales activities, campaigns, leads, and follow-ups.
- **Analytics:** Monitor relevant commercial metrics, including order activity, product performance, and retailer purchasing patterns.

### 2. Product–Retailer Intelligence

Help distributors identify where their existing product portfolio may have untapped opportunities.

- **Assortment Penetration:** Examine how widely individual products are purchased across eligible retailers.
- **Opportunity Identification:** Flag products with limited penetration for further investigation.
- **Retailer Matching:** Identify retailers who may be suitable for particular products based on available purchasing history, product relationships, and business rules.
- **Opportunity Prioritisation:** Rank potential product–retailer combinations using relevant factors such as suitability, stock availability, pricing, and previous purchases.
- **Performance Evaluation:** Compare recommendations and sales outcomes over time as sufficient data becomes available.

Low penetration is a signal to investigate, not proof of demand. Recommendations should account for product suitability, availability, and commercial constraints rather than indiscriminately pushing every product to every retailer.

### 3. Sales Conversion and Follow-up

Help sales teams focus their limited time on actionable opportunities.

- **Lead Management:** Organise prospective and existing retailer opportunities.
- **Follow-up Tracking:** Monitor outstanding sales tasks and relevant customer interactions.
- **Campaign Management:** Coordinate targeted promotions and product offers.
- **Sales Activity Visibility:** Help managers understand recorded sales activities and follow-up progress.
- **Opportunity Execution:** Connect promising product recommendations to salesperson outreach and retailer ordering.

Sales conversion tools help teams execute and follow up on opportunities. Product–retailer intelligence helps identify what may be worth selling to whom. They serve related but distinct purposes.

### 4. Private B2B Retailer Marketplace

A digital ordering channel that connects participating distributors with their authorised retail customers.

- **Product Discovery:** Let retailers browse the products available through their relevant distributor.
- **Distributor-Specific Catalogs:** Present products and applicable prices according to the retailer's access and commercial arrangements.
- **Carton and Bulk Ordering:** Support the bulk-purchasing needs of FMCG retailers.
- **Direct Ordering:** Allow retailers to submit orders digitally instead of relying entirely on manual order-taking.
- **Order Visibility:** Provide access to relevant order details and status information.

The marketplace is designed to complement distributor relationships, not replace them. Product availability, pricing, and ordering rules remain subject to each distributor's configuration and business policies.

### 5. Analytics and Continuous Improvement

Turn accumulated operational data into insights that can support better commercial decisions.

- **Product Performance:** Understand which products sell well and which have limited penetration.
- **Retailer Purchasing Patterns:** Examine purchase frequency, order history, and product adoption.
- **Sales Opportunity Tracking:** Monitor the progression from identified opportunities to sales activities and orders.
- **Outcome Evaluation:** Assess whether recommendations and campaigns are associated with meaningful commercial results.

The quality of insights depends on data completeness, reliability, and consistent activity tracking. Observed engagement or sales after a recommendation does not, by itself, prove that the recommendation caused the purchase.

## Business Model

Lakupak follows a **distributor-focused SaaS and transaction commission model**.

### 1. Distributor SaaS Subscriptions

Distributors can subscribe to plans suited to their business needs and operational scale.

Potential plan differentiation includes:

- **Free:** Access to essential tools for getting started.
- **Starter:** Core operational tools for smaller distributors.
- **Business:** More advanced sales intelligence, analytics, and commercial management capabilities.
- **Enterprise:** Expanded access management, configuration, and support for larger distribution operations.

The exact feature limits, prices, and subscription entitlements should be defined and validated before commercial launch.

### 2. Marketplace Transaction Commission

Lakupak may charge a commission on eligible orders placed through the marketplace. The proposed structure uses lower commission rates for higher subscription tiers, aligning recurring subscription revenue with marketplace activity.

The commission structure should be clearly disclosed, and its final rates and eligibility rules should be validated against distributor margins, transaction economics, and willingness to pay.

### 3. Value Creation

The business model is designed to align Lakupak's revenue with the value it helps distributors create through:

- Better utilisation of existing product portfolios.
- More focused sales activities.
- Improved visibility into retailer relationships and purchasing patterns.
- A more convenient digital ordering channel.
- Better-informed commercial decisions.

Lakupak's commercial sustainability depends on demonstrating measurable value that justifies the subscription and any applicable transaction fees.

## Technical Architecture

Lakupak's intended architecture connects the distributor dashboard, retailer marketplace, backend services, and commercial data layer.

### Technology Stack

- **Web Dashboard:** Next.js and React for the distributor-facing management interface.
- **Mobile Marketplace:** React Native with Expo for the retailer-facing mobile experience.
- **Backend Services:** Node.js with GraphQL and REST API patterns for application services and data exchange.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for structured operational and commercial data.
- **Real-Time Capabilities:** WebSockets and/or Firebase services where required for supported real-time features.
- **Authentication and Authorisation:** Authentication flows and role-based access controls for appropriate user access.
- **External Integrations:** WhatsApp Business API and other relevant services where configured and supported.
- **Containerisation:** Docker for consistent development and deployment environments.

The stack above describes the intended or stated technology choices. The exact services and integrations used in production should be confirmed against the current repository and deployment configuration.

### Architecture Principles

- **Modularity:** Separate application responsibilities so features can evolve without unnecessary coupling.
- **Data Integrity:** Maintain consistent relationships between distributors, retailers, products, inventory, and orders.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Enforce distributor-level data boundaries across application and database access.
- **Access Control:** Apply authorisation checks on the backend, not only in the user interface.
- **Scalability:** Structure services and data access to support growth in distributors, retailers, products, and transactions.
- **AI Readiness:** Build a reliable commercial data foundation before introducing more advanced AI-driven recommendations and automation.
